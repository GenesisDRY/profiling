KK YOUTH PROFILING SYSTEM
Project and website overview

PURPOSE
-------
This PHP/MySQL web application serves Sangguniang Kabataan (SK) officials
and Katipunan ng Kabataan (KK) youth in Barangay Sikat, Alfonso, Cavite.
It provides public information, KK youth registration and member records,
SK role and meeting management, announcements and events, participation
tracking, reports, budget transparency, and account administration.

TECHNOLOGY AND DATA
-------------------
- Most application data is stored as JSON records in MySQL's app_data table.
- init.php supplies page data. storage.js sends authorized changes to api.php,
  which checks permissions before writing.
- Bootstrap provides the interface; SweetAlert2 provides alerts and
  confirmation dialogs.
- The SK meeting editor uses local Summernote 0.9.1 (Bootstrap 5), jQuery
  3.7.1 on the meeting page only, and local DOMPurify for minutes rendering.

ACCESS AND ROLES
----------------
- Public visitors can view the landing page, SK officials, and announcements.
- KK Member accounts have limited, view-only access.
- Active SK accounts can use official management pages; write permissions
  depend on SK role. The SK Chairperson/Chairman and Secretary have additional
  role-management permissions.
- Public registration submissions remain pending until reviewed by officials.
- APIs enforce permissions for data changes.

MAIN PAGES
----------
index.php
  Public landing page with a KK youth count, current SK officials, upcoming
  activities, and public-safe budget transparency information.

login.php / logout.php / change-password.php
  Sign-in, sign-out, and temporary-password change flow. Login attempts are
  rate-limited.

dashboard.php
  Official overview of members, activities, budget, expenses, and upcoming
  activities.

registration.php
  KK youth profiles, public registration, and member archiving/restoration.
  Youth are eligible from age 15 through 30; members older than 30 are
  automatically archived.

request.php
  Review and manage public registration submissions and KK profile edit
  requests, including date filtering and approval/rejection history.

members.php
  Member list and SK role administration. Each person has one KK member
  record; SK roles reference that member. Roles can be ended or archived.

officials.php
  Public listing of current SK officials, showing public-safe profile details.

announcements.php
  Public announcements and events, with official publishing and cancellation
  controls. Account holders sign in before event sign-up. Officers can mark
  sign-up attendance and record active KK members who attended as walk-ins.

participation.php
  Official view of event sign-ups and attendance, including walk-ins, linked
  member details, filters, and summary counts.

reports.php
  Printable reports for KK members, SK meeting attendance, event participation,
  programs, annual summary, and budget/expenses. Meeting and event attendance
  are reported separately.

sk-management.php
  SK meeting scheduling and records, meeting attendance, and print views.
  The SK Secretary can save draft minutes; only the SK Chairman can approve
  or reopen approved minutes. Minutes are sanitized, audited without storing
  their text in audit entries, and can be included in meeting and attendance
  report printouts.

archives.php
  Searchable archive index for archived KK members, ended/inactive or archived
  SK roles, and cancelled announcements/events. Records are managed or
  restored from their original pages.

settings.php
  Application settings and account administration for authorized officials.

DATA AREAS
----------
Important app_data keys include:
- kkMembers: KK youth/member profiles.
- skRoles: SK roles associated with KK member IDs.
- skAnnouncements: announcements and event definitions.
- skEventRegistrations: event sign-ups and attendance state for sign-ups.
- skEventAttendance: walk-in attendance records.
- skMeetings and skAttendance: SK meetings and their attendance.
- Meeting records also carry private minutes, draft/approved state, and
  updater metadata. Minutes are not included in public bootstrap data.
- skTransparencyBudget and skTransparencyExpenses: budget and expense data.
- kkPendingRegistrations and kkEditRequests: submissions awaiting review and
  their review status.

KEY WORKFLOWS
-------------
EVENT SIGN-UP AND ATTENDANCE
- A sign-up is not proof of attendance; officers record attendance separately.
- New sign-ups from linked accounts are associated with the KK member record.
- Officers can add active KK members who attended as walk-ins. Participation
  and reports count walk-ins as attendance, not as sign-ups.
- The walk-in workflow prevents duplicate attendance for the same member/event.
  Older or unlinked sign-ups cannot be automatically tied to a member.

ARCHIVING
- Archived KK members retain their records and may be restored if eligible.
- Ending an SK role marks it Inactive; explicit role archiving marks it
  Archived. Both appear in Archives.
- Cancelled announcements/events remain available to officials for restoration.

BUDGET TRANSPARENCY
-------------------
Budget and expense summaries appear on the public landing page and dashboard.
Public summaries omit expense recipients and descriptions; officials can view
detailed expense records in Reports.

MEETING MINUTES
---------------
Minutes are edited with the local Summernote Bootstrap 5 editor and sanitized
before saving and rendering. Only the SK Secretary and SK Chairman can edit
draft minutes; only the Chairman can approve or reopen them. The server limits
minutes to 20,000 text characters and writes audit entries for saves, approvals,
and reopens without recording the minutes content.
